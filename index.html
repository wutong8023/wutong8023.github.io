Tongtong Wu | 吴桐桐



Hi there !

I’m a Research Fellow in the Department of Data Science & AI at Monash University. I completed my PhD at Southeast University, co-supervised by Prof. Guilin Qi, Prof. Reza Haffari, and Prof. Yuan-Fang Li.

My research focuses on enabling AI systems to perceive, memorize, reason, and adapt within evolving environments, building on my long-term work in Continual Learning and Knowledge Graphs.

AI systems should evolve continuously while remaining reliable and resource-efficient.
